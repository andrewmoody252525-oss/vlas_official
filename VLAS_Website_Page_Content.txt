2.3 OUR HISTORY

2.3.1 Section Label
Our history

2.3.2 Section Heading
Serving Veterans Since 2010

2.3.3 Body Copy
Founded in 2010, Veterans’ Legal Aid Society has spent more than 15 years working to improve access to legal assistance for veterans and their families.

From its earliest years, VLAS has focused on addressing legal challenges that can profoundly affect the lives and well-being of those who served. Its work has included specialized legal services, coordination with veterans’ organizations, and administration of the Patriot Pooled Income Trust, which helps eligible individuals with disabilities preserve assets while accessing important benefits.

Our history is rooted in service. Our future is focused on making justice more accessible to every veteran and family we can reach.

2.3.4 Milestone
2010–TODAY | A Continuing Commitment to Veterans

From specialized legal services and trust administration to expanded legal consultations and educational outreach, VLAS continues to evolve to meet the needs of Illinois veterans and their families.


2.4 WHAT WE DO

2.4.1 Section Label
What we do

2.4.2 Section Heading
Work organized around meaningful access.

2.4.3 Introductory Copy
Veterans’ Legal Aid Society (VLAS) provides free legal assistance to veterans and their families, helping them navigate challenges that can threaten their homes, livelihoods, financial security, and well-being.

Through direct legal services, in-person and virtual consultations, educational outreach, and partnerships with attorneys and veterans’ organizations, VLAS works to make meaningful access to justice a reality for those who served.

2.4.4 Service 01: Ask a Lawyer / Legal Advice Desk
Free in-person and virtual legal consultations are available to veterans and their families.
- Identify legal issues
- Understand available options
- Obtain appropriate assistance or referrals

2.4.5 Service 02: Healthcare Power of Attorney Workshops
Educational workshops support veterans and their families as they plan for healthcare decisions.
- Understand healthcare decision-making
- Prepare important legal documents
- Avoid preventable complications

2.4.6 Service 03: Patriot Pooled Income Trust
Through its role as co-trustee, VLAS helps administer a specialized trust for eligible individuals with disabilities.
- Preserve assets
- Maintain access to Medicaid benefits
